OSIRIS & APIS

Two free apps for automatic crypto investing on Celo, built right into your MiniPay wallet.

OSIRIS — the vault system

OSIRIS is a savings app for crypto. Set up a plan once, and it buys a mix of Bitcoin, Ethereum, Celo, or gold for you automatically — either on a schedule you pick (every hour, day, or week), or the moment a price you set is reached. Your money always stays in your own vault. No one else can move it, not even us.

APIS — talk to your AI assistant

APIS lets you set up and manage OSIRIS without opening the app at all. Just talk to your AI assistant — Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok — and it can check your balances and put together a plan for you. Nothing happens until you personally approve it in MiniPay.

Every trade is routed through Squid Router, which compares many exchanges on Celo at once to find you a fair price.
