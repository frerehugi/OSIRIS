How it works

Set it once, invest automatically

OSIRIS handles the complexity of recurring crypto investments. You define the plan, the vault executes it on schedule — no manual swaps, no missed entries.

Step 1

⚙️

Configure your plan

Choose total amount, token allocation, and interval. Hourly, daily, or weekly — you decide.

Step 2

🔒

Your own vault is created

A dedicated smart contract vault is deployed just for you. Your USDC is non-custodial — only you can cancel and withdraw.

Step 3

🔄

Keeper executes

An automated keeper fetches optimal routes via Squid Router and triggers each swap on schedule.

Step 4

👛

Assets in your wallet

wBTC, wETH, CELO and XAUoT arrive directly in your wallet after every execution.

Architecture

Your own vault, powered by Squid Router

A factory contract creates a dedicated, non-custodial vault per user (EIP-1167 clones — cheap to deploy, fully isolated). Squid is the exclusive routing layer, giving access to the best available liquidity across all DEXs on Celo, including Tether Gold (XAUoT).
