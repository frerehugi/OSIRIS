How OSIRIS works

Set it once, invest automatically

OSIRIS handles the complexity of recurring or price-timed crypto investing. You define the plan, the vault executes it — no manual swaps, no missed entries, no watching charts.

Step 1

⚙️

Configure your plan

Total amount, allocation, and either an interval (hourly to weekly) or a target price.

Step 2

🔒

Your own vault is created

A dedicated smart contract vault deploys just for you. Non-custodial — only you can cancel and withdraw.

Step 3

🔄

Keeper executes

An automated keeper routes each swap via Squid Router — on schedule, or the moment your price is hit.

Step 4

👛

Assets in your wallet

wBTC, wETH, CELO, and XAUoT (or your stablecoins back) arrive directly after every execution.

How APIS works

🐝 Chat your way to a plan

APIS never holds your funds or your keys — it's a conversation layer in front of the exact same OSIRIS contracts above.

Step 1

🔌

Connect your AI

Add APIS as a connector or action in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or Grok — a one-time setup.

Step 2

🔑

Create a one-time code

Generate a signed, time-limited access code in the APIS app — read + propose only, nothing else.

Step 3

💬

Your AI proposes a plan

Paste the code into your chat. It reads your balances and drafts an OSIRIS plan in plain language.

Step 4

✍️

Confirm in MiniPay

Review the plan and sign it yourself. Nothing moves until you do — ever.

Architecture

Your own vault, powered by Squid Router

A factory contract creates a dedicated, non-custodial vault per user (EIP-1167 clones — cheap to deploy, fully isolated). Squid is the exclusive routing layer, giving access to the best available liquidity across all DEXs on Celo, including Tether Gold (XAUoT).
